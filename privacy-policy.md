# Privacy Policy

**ZahBeauty**
**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

ZahBeauty ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ZahBeauty mobile application (the "App"), which lets clients of ZahBeauty, a natural hair studio based in Babylon, New York, browse services, book appointments, pay deposits, and manage their accounts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Who We Are

ZahBeauty is a natural hair studio located in Babylon, New York. We are the party responsible for the personal information collected through the App. For any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at **zahbeauty3@gmail.com**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the App and provide our services.

**Information you provide directly**

- **Account information:** your name, email address, and phone number, which you provide when you create an account.
- **Booking information:** the services you select, appointment dates and times, add-ons you choose, and the total price of each booking.
- **Reviews:** any star rating, written comment, or improvement feedback you choose to submit after an appointment.
- **Communications:** any messages or requests you send to us by email.

**Payment information**

When you pay a deposit, your payment card details are collected and processed directly by **Stripe**, our third-party payment processor. We do **not** receive or store your full card number, security code, or other sensitive card data. We retain only a record that a deposit was paid and the amount paid. Stripe's handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy, available at https://stripe.com/privacy.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Push notification tokens:** if you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can send you appointment reminders and booking updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create, maintain, and secure your account.
- Schedule, confirm, and send reminders about your appointments.
- Process deposits through Stripe.
- Display your reviews within the App and help the studio improve its services.
- Contact you about your bookings, by phone, text message, or push notification.
- Keep accurate business records, including appointment history and revenue.
- Respond to your questions and requests.
- Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share it for third-party advertising or marketing.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by law, we process your personal information on the following bases: to perform our contract with you (for example, to manage your bookings); with your consent (for example, to send push notifications); to comply with legal obligations (for example, tax and accounting records); and for our legitimate business interests (for example, keeping accurate records and preventing fraud), provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

## 5. How Your Information Is Stored and Protected

Your data is stored using **Supabase**, a secure cloud database and authentication provider that hosts the information on our behalf. We protect your data through several measures:

- **Access controls.** Row-level security rules ensure you can access only your own information, and only authorized studio administrators can access booking and client records.
- **Password protection.** Passwords are hashed using industry-standard methods and are never stored in plain text. We cannot see your password.
- **Encryption in transit.** Information sent between the App and our servers is encrypted.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only with the service providers that make the App work, and only as needed for them to perform their services:

- **Supabase**, for database, authentication, and hosting.
- **Stripe**, for payment processing.
- **Expo**, for delivery of push notifications.

These providers process your data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your personal information.

We may disclose information if we are required to do so by law, court order, or governmental request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of ZahBeauty, our clients, or others.

---

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have direct control over your data within the App:

- **Access and export your data.** From your profile, you can download a copy of your personal data, including your profile, bookings, and reviews, at any time using the "Download My Data" option.
- **Correct your information.** You can update your account details from your profile.
- **Suspend your account.** You can temporarily pause your account and reactivate it later by signing back in. Your data is preserved while suspended.
- **Delete your account.** You can request account deletion from your profile. When you do, your account enters a 30-day grace period, during which you can cancel the deletion by signing back in. After 30 days, your profile, account login, and reviews are permanently deleted. Your past bookings are anonymized, meaning they are stripped of any link to you and kept only as non-personal business records (service, date, and amount) for the studio's accounting.
- **Turn off notifications.** You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.

To exercise any right that is not available directly in the App, email **zahbeauty3@gmail.com** and we will respond within a reasonable time. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). These include the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your data. The tools described above are available to all users regardless of location.

We may retain certain records longer where necessary to comply with legal obligations, such as tax or accounting requirements, to resolve disputes, or to enforce our agreements.
